Welcome!

There are many dogs and animals living in our campus. Most of the time nobody notices them. Some of them are hurt, sick or hungry and we walk past without seeing. Campus Paws is a small website made by students so that everyone can see the campus animals and report when something is wrong.

What can you do here?

  • See all the dogs that live in the campus with their name, age, gender and location.
  • Upload a photo if you see a dog that is injured, sick or in trouble.
  • Write what the problem is so that volunteers can help.
